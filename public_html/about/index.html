Technology expertise. People who make it work.

AR TECH INC brings IT consulting, engineering and technology staffing together to help organizations build, connect and maintain their business systems. Our focus is practical progress: useful applications, accessible information and the expertise to move the work forward.

Technology work rarely ends at one system or one skill. Our offering spans cloud, data, software, enterprise platforms and IT support, with project and staffing options that fit the capacity already in your organization.

A connected view of delivery.

  • Cloud & DevOps, Data & AI, Software Engineering, Managed IT & Security, Enterprise Platforms for the systems behind your business.
  • Staff Augmentation, Direct Hire, Dedicated Remote Teams around your technical requirements.
  • A shared delivery plan with clear scope, specialist skills and practical handover.
