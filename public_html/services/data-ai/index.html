Data & AI

What we deliver

Shared information for better reporting and practical AI.

Data engineering & integration

Bring application, database and file data into repeatable pipelines. Ingestion, transformation and validation checks make refreshes easier to maintain and investigate.

ETL, ELT & lakehouse modernization

Modernize data movement and storage around your reporting needs. We map existing transformations, design warehouse or lakehouse models and reconcile migrated data.

Data management & quality

Give reports a dependable foundation. Define data models, validation rules and lineage so teams can trace a metric to its source and investigate inconsistencies.

Business intelligence

Build dashboards around the questions people need to answer. Shared metric definitions, refresh schedules and role-based access help users interpret the same information consistently.

Applied AI & automation

Evaluate focused uses such as document retrieval, classification and workflow assistance. Test representative tasks, define human review and document limitations before wider use.

AI deployment foundations

Integrate the model with the data and applications it needs. Plan access, monitoring and evaluation so the operating team can review behavior as inputs and requirements change.

Delivery process

From assessment to implementation.

Review the assessment, proposed solution, implementation and operating handover at each stage.

Discover

Prepare

Develop

Review
