Engagement models

Choose how to strengthen your team.

Add capacity under your managers, recruit a permanent employee or coordinate a remote team around a project.

Staff Augmentation

Add a specialist to your existing team when a project needs skills or capacity you do not have in-house. Your managers direct the day-to-day activity; the engagement defines the role, duration and working arrangement.

Best suited to
An established team with a specific technical skill or capacity gap.

  • Essential skills and relevant project experience
  • Expected duration and collaboration hours
  • Manager, system access and onboarding requirements

Direct Hire

Recruit a permanent employee for a long-term technology need. A focused role profile and agreed selection criteria guide the search, while your organization makes the hiring decision and establishes employment terms.

Best suited to
A permanent role supporting long-term team development.

  • Responsibilities and essential selection criteria
  • Interview stages and hiring decision makers
  • Location and employment arrangement

Dedicated Remote Teams

Bring several technical roles together for a project that needs coordinated expertise. Define the team lead, responsibilities and collaboration arrangements so the remote team can contribute alongside your organization.

Best suited to
A project or delivery stream involving several technical disciplines.

  • Required roles and project deliverables
  • Team leadership and progress reviews
  • Collaboration hours and working locations

Delivery process

From assessment to implementation.

Review the assessment, proposed solution, implementation and operating handover at each stage.

Define the need

Identify the skill gap, the project or role, and how soon the team needs support.

Shape the role

Separate essential skills from preferences and agree role responsibilities, location and working arrangements.

Review experience

Review relevant project experience against the hiring criteria. Agree interviews and any technical assessment with the hiring team.

Onboard & coordinate

Confirm the start date, system access and reporting line. Set review points with the hiring team.

Hiring brief

Define what the role needs to deliver.

Describe the responsibilities, essential experience and working arrangement. Agree how candidates will be assessed and who makes the selection decision, then prepare the access and onboarding needed for the role.
