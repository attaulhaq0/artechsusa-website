Career path

Data Engineering.

Connect source systems, improve information quality and build the pipelines behind reporting and applied AI. Data engineering brings business questions and technical operations into the same workflow.

Introduce your experience

The work in this discipline

Describe the work you have done and the contribution you made.

  • Develop ingestion, transformation and integration pipelines.
  • Build data models, reconciliation rules and quality checks.
  • Support analytics, reporting and data platform migration.
  • Document lineage, access, refresh schedules and operational monitoring.

What to highlight in your introduction

Describe the work you have done and the contribution you made.

  • Describe a pipeline, integration or data platform you helped build.
  • Highlight Python, SQL, cloud data services and ETL/ELT experience.
  • Explain the validation and reconciliation work behind reliable data.
  • Show how you collaborated with business users, analysts and system owners.

Discuss the right working arrangement.

Role responsibilities, location, collaboration hours, employment terms and selection steps depend on the requirement. A career introduction helps us understand your experience; it does not imply an active vacancy or guarantee placement.
