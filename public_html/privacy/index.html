Who this notice covers

AR TECH INC operates this website. For questions about information submitted through the website, contact hr@artechsusa.com or write to our mailing address: 630 St Andrews Dr APT J206, Mailbox#230, Murfreesboro TN 37128, United States.

Information you provide

An enquiry includes your name, email, company if supplied, topic and message. A career introduction also includes your career interest, optional location and PDF resume. Please provide only information relevant to the discussion and avoid sensitive identification, financial, medical or other unnecessary personal information.

How we use information

We use enquiry information to assess and respond to the requirement you describe. We use career information for recruitment consideration and related communications. Technical information, such as request time and network address, may be used to operate the website, prevent spam and investigate service issues.

Website forms and service providers

Website forms are sent through authenticated email to the company contact mailbox. Hosting and email providers process information as needed to operate those services. The website does not publish uploaded resumes or provide public access to application documents.

Cookies and website assistant

The website uses a short-lived session cookie to protect forms and prevent automated abuse. The guided website assistant runs in your browser to help navigate the site; it does not submit your conversation to a live agent. Optional Google Analytics is loaded only after you select Accept analytics. It measures page visits, browser/device information, successful enquiry and resume submission counts and email-link clicks. We do not send names, email addresses, company names, messages, resume files, query strings or URL fragments to analytics. Advertising features are disabled. Reject analytics leaves these requests off. You can change your choice through Analytics preferences in the footer. Your choice is stored in this browser for up to 180 days; analytics cookies, if accepted, expire after up to 180 days. Google processes this data under its own privacy practices and may process it outside your country. Declining analytics does not affect website forms.

Retention and requests

Information in the company mailbox is retained as needed for the enquiry, recruitment consideration and applicable recordkeeping requirements. You can contact us to ask about your information or request a correction or deletion. We review requests in light of the records held and any applicable obligations.

External links and updates

External websites operate under their own privacy practices. This notice may be updated when the website or its information handling changes. Material changes should be reflected here before a new process is introduced.
