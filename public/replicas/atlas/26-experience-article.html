Make your technical experience easier to understand

Choose relevant examples

Describe the work closest to the roles that interest you. Explain the problem, your contribution and the technologies or responsibilities involved. Be accurate about individual work and work completed by a team.

Make the role context clear

For a published vacancy, read the essential criteria, working arrangement and application route. For a general-interest submission, add a short note about the role families or types of work that interest you. A general-interest submission is not an application to a specific opening.

Check the document

Use a current résumé and confirm that the contact details and document you intend to share are correct. An approved application flow should show accepted file formats and size limits before you select the file.

Share only what is needed

Avoid adding passwords, identity-document numbers, banking information or confidential client material to an initial introduction. Read the approved applicant privacy notice to understand the purpose and handling of the submission.

Understand the next step

Use only an approved vacancy or general-interest route. Submission does not guarantee an opportunity, selection or response date. this website demonstrates the content and interaction; it does not send a real application.
