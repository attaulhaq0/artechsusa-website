FAQs

Questions about the service scope and the candidate journey.

Where should I start with a technology requirement?

Choose the service closest to your need or use Contact with a brief objective, current environment and timing context. Cloud, Data & AI, Custom Software and Managed IT are the four service families in this website.

How do the staffing models differ?

Staff Augmentation adds defined skills to an existing team. Direct Hire addresses a permanent recruitment requirement. Dedicated Remote Teams describes a team structure for agreed work. Management, employment and commercial responsibilities need to be agreed for each engagement.

Can a requirement involve both services and staffing?

Bring the technology scope and specialist skills into one discussion. Explain the systems, delivery requirement and internal capacity so responsibilities can be defined.

What does Managed IT include?

The scope covers user support, monitoring and maintenance for agreed systems. Coverage, response expectations, escalation and ownership are defined in the engagement; this website does not establish a service level.

How do candidates use this site?

Explore technology career paths, review relevant skills and prepare a résumé around the work that interests you.

Are the profiles, scenarios and insights company proof?

Explore our technology services, staffing models and planning insights. Start a conversation around your specific requirements.
